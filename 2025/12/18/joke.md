---

### Why it’s funny

| Element | How it works |
|---------|--------------|
| **Wordplay / pun** | The phrase “make up” has two meanings: 1) to *compose* or *constitute* (atoms are the building blocks of matter), and 2) to *fabricate* or *invent* (as in lying). The joke hinges on the double meaning. |
| **Incongruity** | The listener expects a scientific explanation (“atoms are fundamental particles”) but gets a playful twist that turns a factual statement into a joke about trust and deception. |
| **Surprise / punchline** | The punchline flips the expectation: instead of a technical answer, it delivers a light‑hearted, anthropomorphic take on atoms “lying.” The surprise of the pun is what triggers the laugh. |

So the humor comes from the clever use of a homonym that simultaneously describes the physical nature of atoms and gives them a human‑like, untrustworthy quality.
