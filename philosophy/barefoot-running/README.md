# Philosophy of Barefoot Running

Running on natural and artificial surfaces without artificial support is a way to learn full body motion with only sensory and muscle memory. It is a non-linguistic activity at three levels: neuromuscular, biochemical, and mental.

When run with companions with active conversation, there are linguistic moments in the form of verbal communication in addition to body languages. In solo run, there might be moments of inner dialogues in addition to non-linguistic thoughts. Sensory input plays dominant role in the activity of barefoot running.

Barefoot running is one of the mentally demanding activities that require little linguistic or physical efforts. We can analyze this activity from philosophic standpoint with or without language.

A few weeks ago I bought a pair of sports earbuds and started to listen to [YouTube](/yt) while doing long runs on the tracks and in the park. This _artificial_ experience would only happen with modern technology. The inner dialogues are replaced by the dialogues in YouTube without the visual, the linguistic experience was only auditory (in my head) while the vision stayed in the reality and the body was moving unconsciously most of the time. All the neuromuscular, biochemical, and mental coordinations work smoothly.

This is fascinating.
