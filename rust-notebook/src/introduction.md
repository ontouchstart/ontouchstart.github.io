## What is this notebook then?

This notebook is my resumed exploration of the process of software development with modern toolchains in 
[Python](https://docs.astral.sh/uv) and [Rust](https://doc.rust-lang.org/cargo). 

The investigation in this notebook will be more on **how** to do things with these tools other than **what** to do. 
If I am hired to do things professionally, it would be more about **what** than **how**.

This is notebook of ideas, not a product. 
I will not maintain it professionally because it is not worth it. 
**Please do not use any part of it as a dependency in your project**.

This is not an educational or promotional (portfolio) web site either. I have no interest to teach anyone or explain 
what I did, how I did it and why I did it. Every page, module and line of code has its purpose I might or might not explain.

Feel free to poke around and [contact me](mailto:ontouchstart@gmail.com) if you want to.
