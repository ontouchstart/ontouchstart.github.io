# AI Generated Content 

Keep this in mind: software generated content is not content, it is _only_ data. If we think of AI as software, we must treat AI generated content as _data_.

In the case of Large Language Model, the data you see is nothing but patterns that have high probability in that particular model, plus the prompts generated by you and hidden prompts generated by the system.
The plausibility is just an _illusion_.

This is a hand edited partial list of AI generated content (data) for me to read offline on Kindle and for my bots to analyze. You are welcome to read and share them anyway you like.  

[Always assume AI generated data are nonsense unless you’re convinced some of them make sense to you.](nonsense)
